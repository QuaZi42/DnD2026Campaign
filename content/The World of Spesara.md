## Spesara
Spesara is a world where mortals rose against the Gods seventy years ago. They won, slaying all the Gods. However, instead of inheriting Spesara to govern as their own, the Gods' Children emerged to take their place. The [[Young Gods]] now find that the rules their "parents" abided by were merely suggestions: they meddle with mortals' lives,  fight amongst each other, and do whatever they can to gain more power. 
## History

The World of Spesara existed in a primordial chaos for many epochs. In ~1000 BDE (Before Divine Era) [[Eldath]] happened to form, in the same way a new life form happens to form, like a monkey typing out all of Hamlet after hitting the keyboard for a thousand years. Not much is known about the first couple hundred years of her existence, but it was clear that she was lonely. She put all her divine energy into creating something akin to a child. This time, with Eldath's help, the new life form took much less time to emerge. Around 700 BDE Gond, the future (Old) God of Craft emerged.
