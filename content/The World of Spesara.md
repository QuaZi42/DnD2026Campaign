## Spesara
Spesara is a world where mortals rose against the Gods seventy years ago. They won, slaying all the Gods. However, instead of inheriting Spesara to govern as their own, the [Gods' Children](The%20Young%20Gods.md) emerged to take their place. The [Young Gods](The%20Young%20Gods.md) now find that the rules their "parents" abided by were merely suggestions: they meddle with mortals' lives,  fight amongst each other, and do whatever they can to gain more power. 
## History
### The Creation Of The Universe
The World of Spesara existed in a primordial chaos for many epochs. In ~1000 BDE (Before Divine Era) [[Eldath]] happened to form, in the same way a new life form happens to form, like a monkey typing out all of Hamlet after hitting the keyboard for a thousand years. Not much is known about the first couple hundred years of her existence, but it was clear that she was lonely. She put all her divine energy into creating something akin to a child. This time, with [[Eldath]]'s help, the new life form took much less time to emerge. Around 700 BDE Gond, the future (Old) God of Craft emerged. Together the process of creating new deities went faster and faster. As they did so, the chaotic energy of the world slowly drained from their creations. Each new God became less and less powerful. Around 100 BDE Eldath and Gond (and others) first created Ribbets, who are now the most divine of all the creatures. Next came the Faeries and the Elves. One by one they created all creatures, and once they were satisfied with their creations, used the last of the chaotic energy to create the lands of Spesara for them to inhabit.
