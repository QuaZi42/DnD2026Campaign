# Spesara
**Spesara** is a world where mortals rose against the [Gods](The%20Old%20Gods.md) seventy years ago. They won, slaying all the Gods. However, instead of inheriting Spesara to govern as their own, the [Gods' Children](The%20Young%20Gods.md) emerged to take their place. The [Young Gods](The%20Young%20Gods.md) now find that the rules their "parents" abided by were merely suggestions: they meddle with mortals' lives,  fight amongst each other, and do whatever they can to gain more power. 
## History
### The Creation Of The Universe
The World of Spesara existed in a primordial chaos for many epochs. In ~1000 BDE (Before Divine Era) [[Eldath]] happened to form, in the same way a new life form happens to form, like a monkey typing out all of Hamlet after hitting the keyboard for a thousand years. Not much is known about the first couple hundred years of her existence, but it was clear that she was lonely. She put all her divine energy into creating something akin to a child. This time, with [[Eldath]]'s help, the new life form took much less time to emerge. Around 700 BDE Gond, the future (Old) God of Craft emerged. Together the process of creating new deities went faster and faster. As they did so, the chaotic energy of the world slowly drained from their creations. Each new God became less and less powerful. Around 100 BDE Eldath and Gond (and others) first created Ribbets, who are now the most divine of all the creatures. Next came the Faeries and the Elves. One by one they created all creatures, and once they were satisfied with their creations, used the last of the chaotic energy to create the lands of Spesara for them to inhabit. 
### The Rebellion of The Mortals
Over millennia, the world grew, and the just and loving [[Eldath]] controlled less and less of it. Gods had children and grandchildren, the pantheon stretching thin. Many of the Gods became tyrannical in their rule, demanding more and more sacrifices and waging divine experiments on the mortals. [[Eldath]]'s best attempts at governing the Gods were simply ignored, while the mortals grew weary of the God's dictatorship. 
Finally, the brave mortals, a coalition of all the races of the world, including Ribbets, Elves, Men and Haflings, wielding weapons of tremendous magical potential, rose up and fought the Gods. As unlikely as it was, they won. For a brief moment, it seemed that the Age of Mortals had arrived. Yet quickly soon newborn Young Gods appeared. The Mortals' plan worked partially because they took the Gods by surprise, but now they didn't have that advantage. The spry Young Gods decisively dispelled mortals into obedience.

### Current Day

The Young Gods are best described as "petty assholes." They squabble, give powers to whoever they like, sponsor cults that worship them, change cities' laws, and throw tantrums.
